Privacy Policy

Grammar Lab

Back to support 简体中文

Effective date: October 19, 2026

In one sentence: Grammar Lab does not collect, upload or sell any of your personal data, and does not track you. Your learning records are saved only on your own device.

Data we collect

None. The app requires no registration or login, contains no third-party advertising, analytics or tracking SDKs, and does not connect to any developer server at runtime.

Data stored on your device

To let you continue learning, the app saves the following information locally on your device: learning progress and “studied” marks, practice and review records, experience points and achievements, game records, and preferences such as display zoom. This data does not leave your device, and the developer cannot view it.

You can clear this data at any time in the app under “About → Reset Progress.” Deleting the app also deletes it. If you enable iCloud Backup or a computer backup, this local data is included in your device backup like other app data and is handled by Apple under its privacy policy.

Permissions

The app does not request permissions for your camera, microphone, contacts, photos, location, notifications or advertising tracking.

External links & feedback

When you tap “Privacy Policy,” “Support Page” or “Send Feedback” in the app, the system browser or mail app opens. This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. “Send Feedback” pre-fills the message body with the app version and system version to help diagnose problems; you can edit or remove this information before sending. If you choose to email the developer, the information in your email is used only to reply to you and improve the app, and is not passed on to others.

Services provided by Apple

The app is distributed through the App Store and TestFlight. Apple may collect information such as downloads and crash diagnostics according to your device settings and handle it under Apple’s privacy policy. If you agree to share this information with developers, the developer can see only the anonymous crash reports and aggregate statistics provided by Apple.

Children’s privacy

We do not collect personal information from users of any age.

Policy changes & contact

If data handling practices change, the developer will update this page and the App Store privacy disclosures. For questions, contact dongxiyunei@gmail.com.
