Grammar Lab

Grammar Lab is an offline Japanese grammar app with an English interface. Browse 460 grammar points by JLPT level, from N5 to N1, read explanations and examples, practice with exercises, and review with spaced repetition. The app also includes grammar mini-games and 456 short everyday dialogue clips: one for every N5–N2 grammar point and for 100 of the 104 N1 points.

No account, no ads and no in-app purchases. All content is available offline.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need an internet connection or an account?

No. The grammar content, exercises, example audio and dialogue clips are bundled with the app. You can use them in airplane mode.

Where is my progress saved? Will I lose it when I change devices?

Your progress is saved only on your device and is never uploaded. It is restored when you move to a new device using a full iPhone or iPad backup, through iCloud Backup or a computer backup. Deleting the app removes all progress.

Why can I still hear audio when my device is in silent mode?

Example audio and dialogue clips play only when you tap to play them. The app treats this as media playback, so the silent switch does not mute it. Turn down the volume when you need quiet.

How do I reset my learning progress?

Open About using the button at the top right of the app, then confirm “Reset Progress.” This cannot be undone.

Are the dialogue clips and audio recorded by people?

No. The dialogue visuals and Japanese voices are AI-generated, and the opening frames of scenes and characters were made with an AI image model. Example audio is AI-synthesized speech, made with bilibili IndexTTS 2.5 using the developer’s own voice as a reference. The developer wrote and edited the grammar explanations, examples, exercises and dialogue scripts with AI tools. The app does not call any AI at runtime; all content is prepared in advance. AI-generated content may contain an occasional pronunciation or subtitle error. Please report these by email so the developer can correct them in a future version.

Which devices are supported?

iPhone and iPad running iOS / iPadOS 15 or later, and Macs with Apple silicon.
